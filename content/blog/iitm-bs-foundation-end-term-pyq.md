## How the End Term fits into a Foundation term

Every Foundation course has the same rhythm. There are weekly assignments, then **Quiz 1**, then **Quiz 2**, and finally the **End Term** exam. The End Term covers the full course, all 12 weeks. Quizzes cover only part of it.

The exact grading formula is different for each course. It is published on each course page on the official site. Check it for your courses on the [IIT Madras BS academics page](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/academics.html) before you plan your marks.

> [!NOTE]
> The question counts in this post come from the papers on BTechi only, not from every paper IIT Madras has ever set. They are real counts, not estimates. But treat them as a guide to what IITM likes to ask, not a promise of what will come next.

**Solved example 1 (End Term, Apr 2025 AN).** Let A and B be 4 × 4 real matrices with A² = 2I and B² = 3I. If det(A) < 0 and det(B) > 0, find det(AB).

- det(A²) = det(A)² and det(2I) = 2⁴ = 16. So det(A)² = 16 and det(A) = −4 (it is negative).
- det(B)² = det(3I) = 3⁴ = 81. So det(B) = 9.
- det(AB) = det(A) × det(B) = **−36**.

The trap is writing det(2I) = 2. For an n × n matrix, det(kI) = kⁿ.

**Solved example 2 (End Term, Apr 2025 AN).** Let A = [[7, 3], [4, 3]]. Find det(A⁶ − A⁵).

- Factor first: A⁶ − A⁵ = A⁵(A − I).
- A − I = [[6, 3], [4, 2]]. Its determinant is 6 × 2 − 3 × 4 = 0.
- So det(A⁶ − A⁵) = det(A)⁵ × 0 = **0**.

Do not compute A⁶. Factor, and the question takes 30 seconds. Practise this paper in full: [Maths 2 End Term April 2025 afternoon with answer key](/pyqs/foundation/mathematics-for-data-science-2/end-term-apr-2025-afternoon).

**Solved example 3 (End Term, Apr 2025 FN).** A software company collects 200 bug reports. 50 are critical. It uses a Beta(1.4, b) prior for p, the chance that a report is critical. The prior mean of p should be 0.2. Find b, then the posterior.

- The mean of Beta(α, β) is α / (α + β). So 1.4 / (1.4 + b) = 0.2, which gives 1.4 + b = 7 and **b = 5.6**.
- Posterior = Beta(α + successes, β + failures) = Beta(1.4 + 50, 5.6 + 150) = **Beta(51.4, 155.6)**.

The common mistake is adding 200 (all reports) to β. Only the 150 non-critical reports go to β. Try the full paper: [Stats 2 End Term April 2025 forenoon with answer key](/pyqs/foundation/statistics-for-data-science-2/end-term-apr-2025-forenoon).

**Solved example 4 (End Term, Aug 2025 FN).** Which statements are true about this code? (Select all that apply.)

```python
def safe_divide(x, y):
    result = []
    try:
        result.append("Trying division")
        result.append(x // y)
    except ZeroDivisionError:
        result.append("Division by zero")
    else:
        result.append("No exception occurred")
    finally:
        result.append("Cleanup done")
    return result

print(safe_divide(10, 0))
```

Trace it line by line:

1. "Trying division" is appended first.
2. `10 // 0` raises ZeroDivisionError, so nothing is appended for the division.
3. The `except` block runs and appends "Division by zero".
4. The `else` block runs only when there is no exception, so it is skipped.
5. `finally` always runs and appends "Cleanup done".

Output: `['Trying division', 'Division by zero', 'Cleanup done']`. True statements: the output has "Trying division", it has "Division by zero", and "Cleanup done" always appears. The division result is **not** in the output.

## A 3-week plan to use Foundation End Term PYQs

This plan works for any Foundation course. Adjust the days to your load.

1. **Week 1: top topics only.** Open your course's two or three biggest topics from the tables above. Do the topic sheet for each. Mark every question you got wrong.
2. **Week 1, end: most repeated sheet.** Do the course's most repeated sheet. These are the question types IITM sets again and again.
3. **Week 2: one full paper every two days.** Sit an End Term paper in BTechi's timed [exam portal](/practice). Start with the oldest (Dec 2024). Keep Aug 2025 for last.
4. **Week 2, each paper: review.** Check the official answer key. For each wrong answer, write one line: what was the mistake? Wrong formula, wrong reading or a careless slip?
5. **Week 3: the newest papers.** Sit the Aug 2025 FN and AN papers as a final mock, in one sitting each.
6. **Week 3, last days: your error list.** Re-solve only the questions you got wrong. Do not start new topics.

To print questions, use each course's sheets page, such as the [Maths 2 sheets](/pyqs/foundation/mathematics-for-data-science-2/sheets) and the [Stats 2 sheets](/pyqs/foundation/statistics-for-data-science-2/sheets). Year-wise sheets also exist, such as the [Maths 2 2025 sheet](/pyqs/foundation/mathematics-for-data-science-2/sheets/year-2025) and the [Python 2025 sheet](/pyqs/foundation/programming-in-python/sheets/year-2025). The [PYQ sheets hub](/pyqs/sheets) lists them all.

> [!TIP]
> Sit FN and AN papers of the same day on different days. They often share patterns, so doing them back to back makes the second one feel easier than the real exam will.

When you move on to the next level, the same method works. See our guide to [IITM BS diploma PYQs and important topics](/blog/iitm-bs-diploma-pyq-important-topics). For the full PYQ library across all levels, read [IIT Madras BS data science PYQ: every paper, by level](/blog/iit-madras-bs-data-science-pyq).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I download IITM BS Foundation end term PYQ PDF for free?

BTechi gives every End Term paper as an online paper with the official answer key, plus printable question sheets. Open your course from the [Foundation PYQ page](/pyqs/foundation), then use the paper page to practise online or the sheets page to print.

### Which Foundation End Term PYQs are available for Maths 2, Stats 2, Python and English 2?

Maths 2 and Python have 6 End Term papers each (Dec 2024, Apr 2025 and Aug 2025, FN and AN). Stats 2 has 5 and English 2 has 4. Each course also has at least one quiz paper. The full list is on each course page, for example [Maths 2 PYQs](/pyqs/foundation/mathematics-for-data-science-2).

### What are the most important topics for the Maths 2 End Term?

In BTechi's tagged papers, partial and directional derivatives (41 questions) and critical points with the Hessian test (31) lead by far. Together they are 38% of End Term questions.

### Are End Term questions repeated from previous years?

The same question **types** repeat often, for example the Hessian test in Maths 2 or the Beta posterior in Stats 2. In BTechi's papers, a repeat is usually the same type with new numbers, often in the FN and AN sessions of the same exam. Practise the type, not the answer.

### Is the Foundation End Term harder than the qualifier?

It covers more. The qualifier tests weeks 1 to 4. The End Term tests all 12 weeks, and in Maths 1 and Stats 1 most questions come from weeks 5 to 12.

### Do quiz papers help for the End Term?

Yes, for the first half of the course. Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 papers are good topic practice. But the End Term weights the later weeks more, so do not rely on quiz papers alone.
