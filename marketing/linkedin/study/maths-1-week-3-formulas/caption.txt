IITM Qualifier Maths I week 3 formulas PDF: quadratic functions and equations.

Everything from Maths I week 3 in one carousel: Quadratic functions & parabolas, Quadratic equations & roots.

Key formulas:
Standard form: f(x) = ax² + bx + c, a ≠ 0
Vertex x: h = −b/2a
Vertex y: k = f(h) = −D/4a = c − b²/4a
Axis of symmetry: x = −b/2a
Vertex form: f(x) = a(x − h)² + k
Opens: a > 0: up, minimum = k · a < 0: down, maximum = k
Range: a > 0: [k, ∞) · a < 0: (−∞, k]
y-intercept: (0, c)

Common trap: The max/min value is k (the y-coordinate). The input that gives it is h. Read which one is asked.
