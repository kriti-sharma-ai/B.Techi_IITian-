IITM Qualifier Stats I week 4 formulas PDF: association between two variables.

Everything from Stats I week 4 in one carousel: Two categorical variables, Scatter plots & covariance, Correlation coefficient (r), Point bi-serial correlation.

Key formulas:
Joint relative frequency: cell/(grand total)
Row relative frequency: cell/(row total)
Column relative frequency: cell/(column total)
Population covariance: (Σ(xᵢ − μₓ)(yᵢ − μ_(y)))/n
Scaling: cov(ax + b, cy + d) = ac · cov(x, y)
With itself: cov(x, x) = Var(x)
Pearson r: r = (cov(x, y))/(sₓ s_(y))
Range: −1 ≤ r ≤ 1

Common trap: PYQs ask both kinds: 'population covariance' ⇒ ÷ n, 'sample covariance' ⇒ ÷ (n − 1).
