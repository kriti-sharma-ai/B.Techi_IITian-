## How the Diploma level is organised

In the BS in Data Science, the Diploma level has two diplomas. You can do either one or both:

- **Diploma in Programming:** Database Management Systems (DBMS), Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms using Python (PDSA), Modern Application Development I and II (MAD 1, MAD 2), Programming Concepts using Java, and System Commands.
- **Diploma in Data Science:** Machine Learning Foundations (MLF), Machine Learning Techniques (MLT), Machine Learning Practice (MLP), Business Data Management (BDM), Business Analytics (BA), and Tools in Data Science (TDS). BTechi also has a Quiz 1 paper for Introduction to Deep Learning and Generative AI.

The BS in Management and Data Science shares many of these exams. It also has its own Diploma courses, such as Corporate Finance and Managerial Economics. The official course lists and rules are on the [IIT Madras BS academics page](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/academics.html). Always check them there, because course lists change between batches.

### Business Data Management (BDM)

BDM questions are case-based calculations. The top topics are [Excel tools and charts](/pyqs/diploma/business-data-management/sheets/topic-excel-and-visualisation) (15), [inventory and supply chain](/pyqs/diploma/business-data-management/sheets/topic-inventory-management) (15) and [manufacturing and OEE](/pyqs/diploma/business-data-management/sheets/topic-manufacturing-oee) (14). Repeats: market share from a sales table (5), total revenue (4), and OEE (4).

**Solved example 1 (BDM End Term, Aug 2025 FN).** Planned production time is 480 hours and 48 hours are lost. Design speed is 200 units per hour and actual speed is 180. The plant made 77,760 units, of which 74,772 are good. Find OEE.

- Availability = (480 − 48) / 480 = 432 / 480 = **90%**.
- Performance = actual speed / design speed = 180 / 200 = **90%**.
- Quality = good units / total units = 74,772 / 77,760 = **96.16%**.
- OEE = 0.90 × 0.90 × 0.9616 = **77.9%**. The official key accepts 77 to 78.

Check: 432 hours × 180 units per hour = 77,760 units, which matches. Try the full [BDM End Term Aug 2025 forenoon paper](/pyqs/diploma/business-data-management/end-term-aug-2025-forenoon) and the [BDM most repeated sheet](/pyqs/diploma/business-data-management/sheets/most-repeated).

## Diploma in Programming PYQ: important topics

### PDSA

PDSA questions are spread across many topics, but six lead: [trees, BST and AVL](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/topic-trees-bst-avl) (18), [graphs, BFS, DFS and topological sort](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/topic-graphs-bfs-dfs) (18), [dynamic programming](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/topic-dynamic-programming) (14), [greedy algorithms](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/topic-greedy-algorithms) (14), complexity (14) and sorting (14).

Repeats: relate edges and vertices with n(n − 1)/2 (6), Huffman code lengths (6), Dijkstra's visit order (5), valid LP constraints for a word problem (5), and the possible quicksort pivot (4).

**Solved example 2 (PDSA End Term, Aug 2025 FN).** An undirected graph has 300 edges. What is the minimum number of vertices it must have?

- A simple undirected graph with n vertices has at most n(n − 1)/2 edges.
- We need n(n − 1)/2 ≥ 300, so n(n − 1) ≥ 600.
- n = 24 gives 24 × 23 = 552, too small. n = 25 gives 25 × 24 = 600. So the answer is **25**.

Practise more in the [PDSA most repeated sheet](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/most-repeated) and the [PDSA End Term Aug 2025 forenoon paper](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/end-term-aug-2025-forenoon).

### System Commands

System Commands is about the Linux shell. Top topics: [regular expressions and grep](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/topic-regex-grep) (16), [awk, sort and text filters](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/topic-awk-text-filters) (15), [Bash scripting](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/topic-bash-scripting) (14) and [shell expansion and redirection](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/topic-shell-expansion-redirection) (14). The most repeated type is reading a [crontab entry](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/topic-cron-scheduling) (4 times).

**Solved example 3 (System Commands End Term, Aug 2025 AN).** For the crontab entry `*/12 * * * *`, how many times will the job run in a day?

- The five fields are minute, hour, day of month, month and day of week.
- `*/12` in the minute field means every 12 minutes, starting at 0: minutes 0, 12, 24, 36 and 48. That is 5 runs per hour.
- The hour field is `*`, so this happens every hour: 5 × 24 = **120** runs a day.

The trap is reading `*/12` as "12 times an hour". It is a step, not a count. Practise the full [System Commands End Term Aug 2025 afternoon paper](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/end-term-aug-2025-afternoon) and the [System Commands most repeated sheet](/pyqs/diploma/system-commands/sheets/most-repeated).

## How to use Diploma PYQs: a 4-step method

Diploma courses are heavier than Foundation courses, and most students take two to four at once. A fixed method saves time.

1. **Pick the top three topics.** Use the tables above. Do the topic sheet for each one first, untimed, with notes open.
2. **Do the most repeated sheet.** These are the question types with the best return on time. Aim to get every one right.
3. **Sit full papers under time.** Use BTechi's [exam portal](/practice). Start with Dec 2024 papers and keep the newest Aug 2025 papers for the last week.
4. **Review with the answer key.** For each wrong answer, write the cause in one line. Then re-solve only those questions in the last two days.

For printing, each course has year-wise sheets too, for example the [MLT 2025 sheet](/pyqs/diploma/machine-learning-techniques/sheets/year-2025) and the [PDSA 2025 sheet](/pyqs/diploma/programming-data-structures-and-algorithms/sheets/year-2025). The [PYQ sheets hub](/pyqs/sheets) lists every sheet. To browse papers by term instead, use the [May 2025 term page](/pyqs/term/may-2025) (Aug 2025 End Terms and Quiz 2) or the [January 2025 term page](/pyqs/term/january-2025) (Apr 2025 End Terms).

> [!TIP]
> Forenoon and afternoon papers from the same End Term day often share question patterns. Sit them on different days, so the second one is still a fair test.

> [!WARNING]
> Some courses, especially the coding ones, also have programming exams or projects that are not in these papers. Check each course page on [study.iitm.ac.in](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/) for the full assessment rules. PYQs prepare you for the written exams only.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I download IITM BS Diploma PYQ PDF for free?

BTechi gives each Diploma paper as an online paper with the official answer key, plus printable question sheets. Open your course on the [Diploma PYQ page](/pyqs/diploma). From there you can sit a paper online or open the sheets page to print.

### Which Diploma course has the most previous year papers on BTechi?

### What are the most important topics for MLT?

In BTechi's tagged papers, support vector machines (26 questions), PCA and kernels (17), and Naive Bayes and generative models (14) lead. Hand-run algorithms like the perceptron and AdaBoost repeat often.

### Are Diploma End Term questions repeated?

Question types repeat, often with new numbers. For example, precision from a confusion matrix appears 8 times in BA papers, and the crontab question 4 times in System Commands. Practise the type, not the exact answer.

### Are Diploma in Data Science and Diploma in Programming exams the same for BS in Management and Data Science students?

Many Diploma exams are shared between the programmes, and BTechi marks those courses as having the same quizzes and End Term exams. Check your own course list on the official academics page.

### How many Diploma PYQ papers should I solve per course?

Solve every End Term paper available for your course, under time. For most courses that is 3 to 6 papers. Do the topic sheets and the most repeated sheet first.

### Is there topic data for Programming Concepts using Java?

Not yet. Java papers are on BTechi with answer keys, but the questions are not topic-tagged. Practise them as full papers.
