### Why "January 2027" doesn't mean you start in January

This is the single most common confusion about IITM BS admissions, so it's worth a minute.

IIT Madras runs three terms a year, starting in January, May and September. The **qualifier process runs inside a term**, and Foundation courses begin in the **next** term. The September 2026 cycle makes the pattern clear:

| September 2026 cycle | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications closed | 29 September 2026 |
| Qualifier week 1 began | 2 October 2026 |
| Qualifier exam | 15 November 2026 |
| Foundation courses for those who qualify | January 2027 term |

Apply the same logic to the January 2027 cycle: applications close on 29 January, qualifier coursework follows in February, the exam comes after the four weeks, and Foundation courses start in the May 2027 term.

So if your goal was to **start Foundation courses in January 2027**, that was the September 2026 cycle, which has closed. If you apply now, you're aiming for **May 2027**. That's not a bad thing: it gives you more time to prepare.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The qualifier score also counts as **Quiz 1** for the Foundation courses you take in the term immediately after the qualifier. If you qualify in the January 2027 cycle, plan to register for Maths I, Stats I, CT and English I in the May 2027 term to use that head start.

## Which programme should you apply to?

The January 2027 deadline is the same for both data programmes, and so are the qualifier courses and cut-offs. What differs is everything after Foundation:

| | BS in Management and Data Science | BS in Data Science and Applications |
|---|---|---|
| Run by | Department of Management Studies (launched February 2026) | IIT Madras's original online BS (since 2020) |
| Foundation | Maths I, Stats I, CT, English I + Economics, Accounting, Business Statistics, Management | Maths I & II, Stats I & II, CT, Python, English I & II |
| Diploma | Data Analytics for Business + finance, marketing, economics, management | Programming, and Data Science (ML, tools, BI or deep learning) |
| Heads towards | Business analytics, finance, marketing, operations, management | Data science, ML engineering, software and data engineering |
| Full fee before waivers | ₹3,82,000–₹4,46,000 | ₹3,86,000–₹4,50,000 |

If you're interested in how businesses use data to decide things, Management and Data Science is likely the better fit. If you want to build software and machine-learning systems, Data Science is. Our detailed comparison, [Management and Data Science vs Data Science](/blog/iitm-bs-management-vs-data-science), walks through the decision with examples.

You can only be enrolled in one non-campus IITM BS programme at a time, so choose deliberately.

## Am I eligible?

From the official admissions pages:

- **You've passed Class 12** or equivalent, in any stream, at any age. You can apply.
- **You're in Class 12** (you've finished your Class 11 final exams). You can apply and take the qualifier, but you can't register for Foundation courses until you've passed Class 12. Your qualifier pass stays valid for six terms.
- You're expected to have studied **Mathematics and English in Class 10**.
- If you were eligible to appear for **JEE Advanced** (the Management and Data Science page lists 2025 or 2026), you can use direct entry and skip the qualifier.

### A special note for Class 12 students

If you're in Class 12 right now, look carefully at the calendar before applying to this cycle. The January 2027 qualifier weeks and exam are expected to fall in **February–March 2027**, which is exactly when most school boards hold their Class 12 exams.

You have three reasonable options:

1. **Apply now anyway** if your board timetable leaves room and you're confident you can handle both. The qualifier content overlaps with board maths (sets, functions, straight lines, quadratics), so the work isn't entirely extra.
2. **Wait for the May 2027 cycle**, when your boards are over and you can give the qualifier your full attention. You'd still be ready for Foundation courses in the September 2027 term.
3. **Check JEE Advanced eligibility.** If you're eligible to appear for JEE Advanced, direct entry may be simpler.

Our honest view: board exams matter more for most students' immediate future. Don't put them at risk for a qualifier you can take three months later.

## What it costs

### Application fee

| Category | Application fee | Qualifier re-attempt fee |
|---|---|---|
| General / OBC | ₹4,000 | ₹2,000 |
| SC / ST / PwD (≥40%) | ₹2,000 | ₹1,000 |
| SC / ST and also PwD | ₹1,000 | ₹500 |

There's an extra exam facilitation fee if you take the qualifier exam outside India. The application fee isn't refundable, even if you miss the exam.

### What comes after

Once you qualify, you pay **per course**, only for what you register for each term. For the Management and Data Science programme, Foundation courses cost ₹1,500 per credit, about ₹6,000 for a typical four-credit course, and the whole Foundation level is ₹48,000 before waivers. The Data Science programme's Foundation level is also ₹48,000 under the fee structure that applies from the January 2026 term.

Fee waivers of 50% or 75% are available based on category and family income. Our [complete guide](/blog/iit-madras-bs-degree-complete-guide#fees-what-you-ll-actually-pay) has the full waiver table and the documents you'll need.

## The qualifier cut-offs for January 2027

These are the same on both programme pages for the January 2027 batch.

| Category | Assignments (best 2 of first 3, each course) | Exam (each course) | Exam (average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | 40% | 40% | 50% |
| OBC-NCL / EWS | 35% | 35% | 45% |
| SC / ST / PwD (≥40%) | 30% | 30% | 40% |
| SC / ST and also PwD | 30% | 30% | 40% |

The rule that catches people is the per-course minimum. You must clear it in **every** course, not just on average. A brilliant 70% average with 38% in one course doesn't qualify. Our [qualifier guide](/blog/iitm-bs-qualifier-exam-guide) shows exactly how to prepare around that.

Two practical tips. First, resize images **before** you open the form; most upload errors are file-size errors. Free tools on your phone can compress a photo to under 150 KB. Second, check that your category certificate is current and in your name exactly as it appears on your ID; mismatched spellings cause delays at verification.

Later, when you register for Foundation courses, you'll also upload one of: your Class 12 mark sheet, a degree certificate or your highest education certificate. Verification takes up to three weeks.

## How to apply, step by step

1. **Decide your programme** (Management and Data Science or Data Science and Applications). You can't apply to both at once.
2. **Open the official programme site** at study.iitm.ac.in and use the *Apply now* button for your programme. Be careful with look-alike sites; the official domain is iitm.ac.in.
3. **Register with a personal email address you'll keep for years.** The FAQ notes that the personal email used for the application can't be changed later.
4. **Fill in your details** exactly as they appear on your ID. You can edit the application until you pay the fee; after that, corrections become difficult.
5. **Choose your exam cities.** You'll pick two preferences, in order. Choose cities you can reach early in the morning without a risky commute. You can change them up to four weeks before the qualifier exam.
6. **Upload documents** in the formats and sizes above.
7. **Pay the application fee** online. The FAQ lists credit card, netbanking, wallet and UPI; debit cards aren't accepted. Wait for the confirmation email from the payment gateway.
8. **Save everything**: your application number, payment confirmation and a PDF of the submitted form.
9. **Watch your email and dashboard** for the qualifier start date and course access.
10. **Start week 1 the day it opens.** Assignments from week 1 count towards Gate 1.

> [!WARNING]
> If a payment fails but money leaves your account, don't pay again immediately. Failed payments are refunded, but the FAQ says this takes three to four weeks. Contact the support email for your programme before retrying.

## What's new for 2027 applicants

A few things make this cycle different from what older blog posts and videos describe.

**A new programme is in the mix.** The BS in Management and Data Science was launched in February 2026, run by IIT Madras's Department of Management Studies. Many guides online still describe only the Data Science programme, so if you're reading older material, check which programme it's about.

**The Data Science fee structure changed.** From the January 2026 term, the Data Science programme lists a revised fee structure (₹48,000 for Foundation; ₹3,86,000–₹4,50,000 for the full BS). Learners who joined up to September 2025 follow the older structure. Fee figures in pre-2026 articles may be out of date.

**The degree ladder is longer.** The Data Science programme now lists optional continuations beyond the BS: a PG Diploma in AI & ML (162 credits) and an MTech in AI & ML (182 credits).

**AI is part of the curriculum.** Management and Data Science includes GenAI for Business as a Degree-level core course, with electives such as Economics of AI and Responsible AI. The Data Science programme's diploma includes a track in Deep Learning and Generative AI. That reflects what employers are asking for: we've written about [how AI is changing analyst roles](/blog/ai-and-data-analyst-jobs-2026).

## A month-by-month plan from now

You have more time than the deadline suggests. Here's how to use it, assuming you apply well before 29 January and the qualifier begins in early February.

### October–November 2026: decide and get comfortable

- Read the curriculum of both data programmes and decide which you're applying to.
- If you haven't done maths since Class 10, spend 30 minutes a day on algebra: linear equations, factorising, the quadratic formula, and coordinate geometry basics.
- Read one English editorial a day, noting unfamiliar words and phrases.

### December 2026: preview the qualifier

- Read BTechi's [cheat notes for all four qualifier courses](/qualifier/notes) once, slowly. Don't memorise; just notice what feels hard.
- Attempt one [previous-year qualifier paper](/blog/iitm-qualifier-pyq-with-answer-key) untimed. Score it **by course** and write down your weakest one.
- Practise tracing simple step-by-step procedures on paper for CT.

### January 2027: apply and warm up

- Apply in the first half of the month, not on the 29th.
- Sort out logistics: a quiet study slot every day, a notebook per course, your exam city choices.
- Spend extra time on your weakest course from December's practice paper.

### February–March 2027: the qualifier weeks

- Follow the week-by-week plan in our [qualifier exam guide](/blog/iitm-bs-qualifier-exam-guide): watch, practise, submit, review, every week.
- Aim for 80%+ on assignments so a bad week doesn't threaten Gate 1.
- In week 4, sit full timed mocks such as BTechi's [mock qualifiers](/qualifier).

### April–May 2027: register and start

- Once you've qualified, register for Foundation courses for the May 2027 term.
- If you're also in college or working, start with two courses, not four.
- Use the gap to start a skill that'll matter later, such as [Python for data analysis](/skills/python-for-data-analysis/notes) or [Excel for business](/skills/excel-for-business/notes).

## Mistakes to avoid this cycle

1. **Applying on the last day.** Websites slow down near deadlines, payments fail, and documents get rejected for size. Apply early.
2. **Assuming you'll start in January.** You'll start Foundation in the May 2027 term if you qualify in this cycle. Plan college, work and travel accordingly.
3. **Using an email you'll lose.** A college email that expires, or a parent's email, causes problems for years. Use your own long-term personal address.
4. **Choosing exam cities casually.** Quiz and exam days are fixed, early, and in person. Pick cities you can reach reliably.
5. **Ignoring the Class 12 board clash.** If you're in school, compare the board timetable with the expected qualifier dates before you pay.
6. **Trusting old fee figures.** Check the current official fee pages; several older articles predate the 2026 changes.
7. **Joining unofficial "admission help" groups that ask for money.** The official FAQ is clear that there are no official WhatsApp or Telegram groups. Applications are made only on the official site.

## After you've applied

Once you've submitted and paid:

- **Look for your confirmation** email and keep your application number handy.
- **Check your dashboard** regularly for course access and qualifier announcements.
- **Bookmark the support contact** for your programme. For the Management and Data Science programme, the official contact is support-mg@study.iitm.ac.in, with a helpline listed on the programme page (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm).
- **Start preparing**, if you haven't already. The four qualifier weeks move fast.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the last date to apply for the IIT Madras BS January 2027 qualifier?

29 January 2027, according to both the Management and Data Science and the Data Science and Applications programme pages, checked in October 2026.

### When is the January 2027 qualifier exam?

The exact date hadn't been published in the material we could check in October 2026. ### If I apply for January 2027, when do classes start?

If you qualify, your first Foundation courses are expected in the May 2027 term. The qualifier itself runs during the January 2027 term.

### Can I apply to both the Management and Data Science and the Data Science programmes?

You can't be enrolled in two non-campus IITM BS programmes at the same time, so choose one. The qualifier courses and cut-offs are the same for both.

### What is the application fee for January 2027?

₹4,000 for General and OBC applicants, ₹2,000 for SC/ST/PwD applicants, and ₹1,000 for applicants who are SC/ST and also PwD. Re-attempt fees for the qualifier exam are ₹2,000, ₹1,000 and ₹500 respectively.

### I'm in Class 12. Should I apply in January 2027?

You can, but the qualifier is expected to overlap with Class 12 board exams in February–March. Many students are better off applying in the May 2027 cycle, after boards. Your qualifier pass is valid for six terms either way.

### Can JEE Advanced candidates join in January 2027 without the qualifier?

Candidates eligible for JEE Advanced (the Management and Data Science page lists 2025 or 2026) can use the direct-entry route: pay the admission fee, submit JEE proof, and register for Foundation courses once it's verified.

### Is there an age limit?

No. Anyone who has passed Class 12 or equivalent can apply, irrespective of age or academic background.

### Can I change my exam city after applying?

Yes, up to four weeks before the qualifier exam, according to the official FAQ.

### What if I miss the 29 January deadline?

Admissions run three times a year. The next cycle after January is expected to be the May 2027 cycle. Check the official site for its dates.
