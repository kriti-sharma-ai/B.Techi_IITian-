IITM Qualifier Stats I week 2 formulas PDF: describing categorical data.

Everything from Stats I week 2 in one carousel: Describing categorical data.

Key formulas:
Relative frequency: f/n
Percentage: f/n × 100
Pie-chart angle: f/n × 360°
Count from a slice: n × angle/360°  or  n × percentage
Mode: the category with the highest frequency
Median (ordinal only): the middle category after ordering

Common trap: Mean makes no sense for categorical data, even when categories are coded as numbers.
