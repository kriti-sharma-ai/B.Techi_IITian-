# LinkedIn plan: 30 days to the IIT Madras BS Qualifier

**Main goal:** BTechi shows up in Google when students search for the Qualifier ("iitm qualifier cutoff", "iitm qualifier maths important questions pdf" and similar).

**Second goal:** Students who are writing the Qualifier find BTechi, try a free mock, and share the posts with friends.

**Audience:** People who are writing the IITM BS Qualifier, their parents, and working people who are joining the BS degree.

## How we post

| | Morning post | Evening post |
|---|---|---|
| Time (IST) | 8:30 AM | 7:30 PM |
| Type | **Teach** carousel: one topic, its formulas and its traps | **Quiz** carousel: one question, answer on the next slide |
| Ask in the caption | "Save this for revision" | "Comment your answer, then swipe" |

- **Start date:** Day 1 is 30 days before the Qualifier. Post the launch carousel (`day-0-launch/`) the day before Day 1.
- **How to upload:** Start a post, choose **Add a document**, upload `carousel.pdf`, paste `title.txt` as the document title, and paste `caption.txt` as the post text. Do not change the first line of the caption. It holds the search phrase.
- **Profile:** Post from your own profile, then reshare it on the BTechi page. Personal posts usually reach more people.
- **First hour:** Reply to every comment. On quiz posts, reply with "Correct" or a short hint. Do not give the answer away.
- **Links:** Every caption has one link to the btechi.in page that should rank for that topic. The link has UTM tags (`utm_campaign=qualifier30`), so visits from each post show up in analytics. Keep the link in the caption, not in a comment, so Google sees it with the post.
- **Hashtags:** Use 5 at most. They are already in each caption.
- **Rules:** Use only real facts and real PYQs from the cheat sheets. No emojis. No fake reviews or fake results. Every example scorecard is marked "Example".

## How this helps Google search

The plain facts:

- **Google can show LinkedIn posts, but it reads only the caption.** It does not read the text inside the PDF slides. So every caption repeats the key points in plain text.
- **The first line matters most.** LinkedIn builds the post's web address and its page title from the first words. Google then uses that title. So every first line starts with the search phrase, for example "IITM Qualifier cutoff explained: ...".
- **Links from LinkedIn do not pass ranking power directly**, because LinkedIn marks them "nofollow". They still help in other ways: visits, people searching for "btechi", and people who find the post and link to btechi.in from their own sites.
- **btechi.in pages are what really rank.** So each post sends people to the one page we want to rank for that topic:

| Topic | Search phrase | Page the posts link to |
|---|---|---|
| Plan and cutoff | iitm qualifier study plan, iitm qualifier cutoff | /blog/iitm-bs-qualifier-exam-guide |
| Maths I | iitm qualifier maths important questions | /blog/iitm-qualifier-maths-important-questions |
| Stats I | iitm qualifier stats important questions | /blog/iitm-qualifier-stats-important-questions |
| Computational Thinking | iitm qualifier computational thinking pyq | /blog/iitm-qualifier-computational-thinking-pyq |
| English I | iitm qualifier english important questions | /blog/iitm-qualifier-english-important-questions |
| Mocks | iitm qualifier mock test | /qualifier |
| Notes | iitm qualifier notes pdf | /qualifier/notes |

What else to do, biggest win first:

1. **Write the missing blog pages.** Your blog plan (content/blog/_plan.md) already has "iitm-qualifier-30-day-study-plan" as next up, and the 4 course guides are still planned. Once each page is live, point that topic's posts to it.
2. **Post one LinkedIn Article each week** (Write article, not Start a post). Google finds articles more easily than posts. Make it a short summary of the week, with links to the blog pages. Do not copy a whole blog post into it, or it can compete with your own page in Google.
3. **Update the BTechi company page.** Put "IIT Madras BS Qualifier mock tests, PYQs and notes" in the tagline and the About section, with the btechi.in link.
4. **Use the same words everywhere.** The post's first line, the PDF title and the blog page title should all use the same search phrase.

- **Folders:** `study/<subject>-week-<n>-<formulas|patterns|rules>/`. Each has `carousel.pdf`, `title.txt` and `caption.txt`, the same as the daily posts.
- **When to post:** one study carousel each Saturday and Sunday, on top of the 2 daily posts. Pick the subject that week's posts covered. Or post them on the BTechi company page while the daily posts go on your profile.
- **Google search:** each caption starts with "IITM Qualifier <subject> week <n> <formulas> PDF". It lists the key formulas in plain text, because Google does not read the slides. It links to that subject's notes page, `/qualifier/notes/<subject>`, with `utm_campaign=study-notes`.
- **When the notes change:** run `node scripts/generate-carousel.mjs -week-` to rebuild all 16 from the latest cheat sheets.
- **Note:** For CT (all weeks) and English (weeks 3 and 4), the week in each cheat-sheet section does not match the week titles in `src/lib/data/qualifier-meta.ts`. The carousels follow the cheat sheets. One of the two lists on the website may need fixing.

## How to make the next carousels

1. Copy `scripts/carousel/week-1.mjs` to `scripts/carousel/week-2.mjs`. Write the posts using the same slide types: `cover`, `list`, `bullets`, `formulas`, `question`, `answer`, `scorecard`, `stats`, `statement` and `cta`.
2. Take facts and PYQs only from `src/lib/data/cheat-notes/`. Add an entry for each post in the `SEO` list at the top of the file (search phrase, first line, PDF title, page to link).
3. Add the new week in `scripts/generate-carousel.mjs`, then run:

```bash
node scripts/generate-carousel.mjs            # all posts
node scripts/generate-carousel.mjs day-08     # only one day
```

4. To check the slides before posting, add `--preview <folder>`. It saves one contact-sheet image per post. If a slide has too much text, the script prints a warning.
