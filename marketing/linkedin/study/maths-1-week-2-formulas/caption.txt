IITM Qualifier Maths I week 2 formulas PDF: coordinate geometry and straight lines.

Everything from Maths I week 2 in one carousel: Coordinate geometry, Straight lines, Straight-line fit.

Key formulas:
Distance: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)
Midpoint: ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
Section, internal m : n: ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m + n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m + n))
Section, external m : n: ((mx₂ − nx₁)/(m − n), (my₂ − ny₁)/(m − n))
Centroid: ((x₁ + x₂ + x₃)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + y₃)/3)
Area of triangle: ½ |x₁(y₂ − y₃) + x₂(y₃ − y₁) + x₃(y₁ − y₂)|
Collinear points: area = 0 (or equal slopes)
Distance from the axes: from x-axis: |y| · from y-axis: |x|

Common trap: In the section formula, m multiplies the second point (x₂, y₂).
