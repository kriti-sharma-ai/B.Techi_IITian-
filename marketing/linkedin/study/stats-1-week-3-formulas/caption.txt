IITM Qualifier Stats I week 3 formulas PDF: describing numerical data.

Everything from Stats I week 3 in one carousel: Numerical data: tables & charts, Mean, median & mode, Range, variance & standard deviation, Percentiles, quartiles, IQR & outliers.

Key formulas:
Class width: upper limit − lower limit
Class mark (midpoint): (lower + upper)/2
Mean of grouped data: (Σ fᵢxᵢ)/(Σ fᵢ)
Mean: x̄ = (Σ xᵢ)/n
Mode: most frequent value (there may be none, or several)
Combined mean: (n₁x̄₁ + n₂x̄₂)/(n₁ + n₂)
Add one value x: new mean = (nx̄ + x)/(n + 1)
Fix a wrong value: new mean = x̄ + (correct − wrong)/n

Common trap: 'Above 23' excludes 23; 'at least 23' includes it.
