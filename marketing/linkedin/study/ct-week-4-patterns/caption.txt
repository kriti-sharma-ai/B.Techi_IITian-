IITM Qualifier CT week 4 patterns PDF: nested loops, binning and tracing.

Everything from CT week 4 in one carousel: Nested iterations: comparing pairs, Binning, Tracing: 'What does A represent?'.

Key patterns:
Unordered pairs (each once): (n(n − 1))/2
Ordered pairs, X ≠ Y: n(n − 1)
Every X with every Y, itself included: n²
Pairs within groups of sizes k₁, k₂, …: Σ (kᵢ(kᵢ − 1))/2
Pairs across two groups: k₁ × k₂
Comparisons with bins: Σ (kᵢ(kᵢ − 1))/2

Common trap: Read whether the question asks for matching pairs or for comparisons made. Comparisons = n(n − 1)/2, whatever the if says.
