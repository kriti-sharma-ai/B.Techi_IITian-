IITM Qualifier Maths I week 4 formulas PDF: polynomials and their graphs.

Everything from Maths I week 4 in one carousel: Polynomials: algebra & division, Graphs of polynomials.

Key formulas:
Degree: highest power with a non-zero coefficient
Degree rules: deg(pq) = deg p + deg q · deg(p ± q) ≤ max(deg p, deg q)
Division algorithm: p(x) = d(x) · q(x) + r(x),  deg r < deg d
Remainder theorem: p(x) ÷ (x − a) leaves remainder p(a)
Factor theorem: (x − a) is a factor ⇔ p(a) = 0
Sum of coefficients: p(1)
Square: (a ± b)² = a² ± 2ab + b²
Difference of squares: a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)

Common trap: 'Distinct roots' counts each root once. Counted with multiplicity, (x − 1)² is two roots.
