IITM Qualifier CT week 2 patterns PDF: filtering, AND / OR, max and min.

Everything from CT week 2 in one carousel: Pseudocode, filtering & AND / OR, Max, min & 'who has it'.

Key patterns:
De Morgan: not (A and B) = (not A) or (not B)
De Morgan: not (A or B) = (not A) and (not B)
Count of A or B: count(A) + count(B) − count(A and B)
Exactly one of A, B: (A and not B) or (B and not A)

Common trap: min = 0 bug: no mark is below 0, so min never changes. A favourite 'find the mistake' option.
