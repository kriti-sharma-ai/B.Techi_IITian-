IITM Qualifier English I week 2 rules PDF: parts of speech and grammar.

Everything from English I week 2 in one carousel: Nouns & pronouns, Verbs, tenses & modals, Adjectives & adverbs.

Key rules:
A as well as / along with / with B: the verb agrees with A
Either … or / neither … nor: the verb agrees with the nearer subject
Each, every, everyone, one of the: singular verb
The number of / a number of: singular / plural
get / have + object + past participle: causative

Common trap: 'Swimming pool' is a compound noun, not adjective + noun.
