IITM Qualifier Maths I week 1 formulas PDF: sets, Venn diagrams, relations and functions.

Everything from Maths I week 1 in one carousel: Number systems & sets, Venn diagrams & counting, Relations, Functions.

Key formulas:
Chain: ℕ ⊂ ℤ ⊂ ℚ ⊂ ℝ
Rational: p/q, p, q ∈ ℤ, q ≠ 0
Irrational: √2, √3, π, e
gcd × lcm: gcd(a, b) × lcm(a, b) = a × b
Power set: |P(A)| = 2ⁿ
Proper subsets: 2ⁿ − 1
Subsets of size k: C(n, k) = n!/(k!(n − k)!)
Cartesian product: |A × B| = |A| · |B|

Common trap: ∈ is for elements, ⊆ is for sets: 1 ∈ {1, 2} but {1} ⊆ {1, 2}.
