IITM Qualifier English I week 3 rules PDF: articles, prepositions and phrasal verbs.

Everything from English I week 3 in one carousel: Articles: a, an, the, Prepositions & conjunctions, Phrasal verbs, idioms & collocations.

Key rules:
a: before a consonant sound
an: before a vowel sound
the: specific: something already known or identified
the: unique: only one exists
the: rank: superlatives and ordinals
the: geography: rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, island groups

Common trap: 'As if' introduces an imagined comparison; plain 'as' means 'in the way that' or 'because'.
