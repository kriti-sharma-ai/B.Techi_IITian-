IITM Qualifier English I week 1 rules PDF: sounds and pronunciation.

Everything from English I week 1 in one carousel: Vowel sounds, Diphthongs, Consonants, clusters & glides.

Key rules:
/w/ glide: after u, uu, ou, au (rounded lips) + a vowel
/y/ glide: after i, ii, ei, ai, oi (spread lips) + a vowel

Common trap: Spelling lies: 'oo' in foot is short u /ʊ/, but in food and root it's long uu /uː/.
